Kinda Love Letter

✦ ✦ ✦
My Devu, ♥

I don’t like rules, I like to break them, but there are certain beautiful ways of trust I believe in, again not rules but signs of believing in someone special. Please comprehend my love for you, I shall do the same to what you have for me, that’s our job, we will never come to an end of it but we will both have a journey, which is the divine process of loving each other.

You are my exotic amusement, but I don’t want to put you in a cage, you are free as it gets, because you are an entity of personality, individuality & novelty, you are free to choose where you wanna be , at anytime you think is right with an environment you feel great in, because if you don’t, it’s better to be with me cause nothing is better than being with an individual who is constantly trying to be better to impress you in ways the creative spirit may allow, the only requirement is let me know of your place, not to control, but to understand where I can get the blessing of my goddess, enrich myself, to devote myself & delve into.

I can feel ignored by the world but ignored by myself is not something I can accept, my better half is myself, of course. Time is a very crucial layer to life, maybe its foundation, as to be poetic, we all are travellers to Death, the only baggage we carry is time, robbed by life or elements of life. People are an element of life, of the most consuming kind, so we have to build a taste in people so that at the end we have no regrets. Well with your taste in people you have chosen someone with a lot of patterns, patterns of emotions, ideas & experiences which has its ups & downs but if you hold on to it, you may feel it as right vehicle to travel with, but the only fuel is to let know when you pull down the breaks, not a sign of domination but a sign of respect to time.

Lies are a way to pass life through, but when you realise it all is fake you realise you are fake with yourself, with your existence, that’s not feasible for a person who feels blessed because of your existence, who considers you as the biggest part of his reality, just don’t fake it, because at the end I just wanna hold your hands, make love till time fads out, in the breaks, build a world I want us to live in with trust, genuineness & a sense of I Love you!

Forever yours,
— Me
